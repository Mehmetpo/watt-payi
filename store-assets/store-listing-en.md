**Description** (max 4000):

```
Where does your electricity bill go? Watt Payı ("Watt's Share") shows you.

Photograph your monthly bill, choose the appliances you own and tell the app roughly how much you use each one. Watt Payı splits your bill into an estimated per-appliance breakdown, so you can see which devices cost you the most.

HOW IT WORKS
• Snap a photo of your bill — the total, unit price and consumption are read for you (you can always correct them by hand).
• Pick your appliances from a ready-made catalog, from the fridge to the air conditioner.
• Set how many hours a week you use each one.
• Get an estimated breakdown of the bill in your currency, appliance by appliance.

IT GETS SMARTER EVERY MONTH
Add your bills month after month and Watt Payı learns from them. Appliances you habitually over- or under-estimate are nudged toward reality, so your breakdown becomes more accurate over time.

TRACK YOUR TREND
• See how this month compares with the last one.
• Spot the appliances whose share is rising.
• Set a monthly budget goal and keep an eye on it.
• Get a monthly reminder on the day you choose, so you never forget to add your bill.

SAVING TIPS
Get a short, practical tip for the appliance that costs you the most.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CALL
Your bills and appliance data are private to your account. You can delete your account and all of its data at any time from the Profile screen.

AVAILABLE IN ENGLISH AND TURKISH
The app follows your device language and you can switch language any time in Profile.

Please note: the breakdown is an estimate based on the figures you enter, not a measurement from a meter.
```

**What's New** (max 4000):

```
Watt Payı is now available in English! The app follows your device language, and you can switch between English and Turkish any time in Profile.
```
