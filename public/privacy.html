Watt Payı Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026 · Türkçe

Watt Payı ("the app") helps you photograph your electricity bill and see an estimated per-appliance breakdown. This page explains which data we process and why.

Data we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password (the password is stored encrypted and used only for authentication).
  • Bill data: the bill photo you take or select, and the total, kWh, unit price and period read from the bill.
  • Usage information: the appliances you select, their weekly usage hours, wattage values, your monthly budget and reminder day.
  • Advertising data: your device's advertising identifier and general usage information used to show ads (see below).

How we use data

  • To split your bill across appliances and show your bill history and trends.
  • To send a monthly reminder notification (a local notification on your device).
  • To generate savings tips.
  • To show the ads that keep the app free.

Who we share data with

  • Supabase: our infrastructure provider, where account, bill and appliance data and bill photos are stored.
  • Anthropic: to read your bill photo and extract the total and kWh values, and to generate savings tips, the photo and related text are sent to Anthropic's AI service. This data is not used for advertising.
  • Google AdMob: to show ads. On iOS, personalized ads are tracked only with your permission. If you decline, you will see less personalized ads.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: bot protection (during sign-in and sign-up).

We do not sell your data.

Tracking (App Tracking Transparency)

On iOS, we show you the system permission prompt before using the advertising identifier. If you decline, or later turn it off under Settings > Privacy > Tracking, the app works exactly the same.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is open. In the app, go to Profile > "Permanently delete my account" to permanently delete your account and all of your data, including bill photos.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, and deletion of your data. You can delete your data yourself from the app; for other requests, write to the address below.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
